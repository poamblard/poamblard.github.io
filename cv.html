Pierre-Olivier Amblard

Director of Research at CNRS

Origins & Early Life

Born on September 25th in Dijon (Côte d'Or, France), capital of the Dukes of Burgundy. Winner of the Côte d'Or football championship with his teammates from ASPTT Dijon during his primary school years at St Joseph.

Personal Life

Married, father of 3 children.

Early Education
Attended Lycée Montchapet in Dijon (Baccalauréat). Entered "Maths Sup" at Lycée Carnot in 1985.
1987 – 1990
Moved to Grenoble for engineering studies at ENSIEG (INPG). Graduated with an Electrical Engineering degree and a Master's degree in Signal Processing in 1990. Simultaneously studied physics at Université Joseph Fourier out of passion.
1990 – 1994
Completed his PhD under the supervision of Daniel Baudois (defended Jan 1994). Served as Associate Lecturer (ATER) at ENSIEG during 1993–1994.
1994 – Present
Joined the CNRS as a researcher. Obtained his Habilitation (HdR) in 2001 and was promoted to Director of Research in 2008.
2010 – 2013
Secondment at the Department of Mathematics and Statistics, University of Melbourne (Australia).
2012 – 2021
Elected member of the Comité National de la Recherche Scientifique (CoNRS) for Section 07 (Signal/Image Processing, Robotics, Control) and Section 51 (Physics & Math applied to Biology). Served as Elected President of Section 07 from 2016 to 2021.

Note on CoNRS: The National Committee for Scientific Research is responsible for evaluating CNRS units and researchers, as well as managing recruitment competitions.
