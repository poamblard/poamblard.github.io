Pierre-Olivier Amblard was born on September 25th, in a an hospital in Dijon, capital of the Burgondy Dukes, Côte d'Or, France. He has 1 wife, and 3 children. After having thoroughly studied in St Joseph primary school and winning the Côte d'Or football championship ( with his friends of the ASPTT team of Dijon), he went to lycée Montchapet where he passed the baccalauréat, without any honor. He then entered these strange school called ''math'sup'' at lycée Carnot of Dijon in 85, before coming to Grenoble for engineering studies at ENSIEG, INPG, in 1987. His madness, or something else, lead him to study physics at university Joseph Fourier (the guy of the series and of the transfom). He obtained an electrical engineering degree in 1990, a master degree in 1990 in signal processing, both from the INP Grenoble. He did his PhD under the supervision of Daniel Baudois (defended in january1994). During the academic year 1993-1994 he was associate lecturer (ATER) at ENSIEG. He entered the celebrated CNRS in 1994 where he works since then. He obtained his HdR (Habilitation à Diriger des Recherches) in 2001. He became Director of Research (Prof) in 2008. From Aug 2010 to July 2013, he was in secondment at the department of mathematics and statistics of the University of Melbourne (Australia, not USA). From 2012 to 2016, he is an elected member of the Comité National de la Recherche Scientifique, in section 07 (signal and image processing, robotics, automatic control, speech processing, ...) and in section 51 (interdisciplinary section : physics and mathematics applied to biology). From 2016 to 2021 he was an elected member of section 07, and he served as the elected president of this committee. CoNRS is in charge of the evaluation of the CNRS units and researchers, in charge of the recruitment competitions.
